Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Duong Thanh Linh ("we", "us") collects, uses and shares information when you play our mobile games, including Draw Car Race (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you for your name, address or phone number. The App and the third-party services it uses may automatically collect the following information:

  • Device information: device model, operating system version, language, screen size and country.
  • Identifiers: the Android Advertising ID, an app instance ID and, where applicable, an IP address.
  • Gameplay and usage data: levels played, session length, in-game events and settings.
  • Crash and performance data: crash logs and diagnostics that help us fix bugs.
  • Purchase information: if you make an in-app purchase, Google Play processes the payment. We receive a confirmation of the purchase but never your card details.
  • Game progress: your progress and settings, stored only on your device. We do not run our own servers and do not upload your progress.

2. How we use information

  • To run the App, save your progress and deliver purchased items.
  • To show advertisements, including personalized ads where permitted.
  • To understand how players use the App and improve gameplay and balance.
  • To detect crashes, fix bugs and prevent fraud or cheating.
  • To respond to your support requests.

3. Third-party services

The App uses the following third-party services. Each one collects information under its own privacy policy:

  • Google Play Services and Google Play Billing (in-app purchases) – Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob (advertising) and the Google User Messaging Platform (consent) – How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • AppsFlyer (install attribution and measurement) – AppsFlyer Privacy Policy
  • Unity (game engine and in-app purchasing) – Unity Privacy Policy

4. Advertising

The App shows advertisements served by Google AdMob, including rewarded video ads that you choose to watch in exchange for in-game rewards. Our ad partners may use your Advertising ID, IP address and device information to show ads, to personalize them where permitted, to measure ad performance and to prevent fraud.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent through Google's consent form before ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time from Settings › Privacy options inside the App. You can also reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Settings › Google › Ads or Settings › Privacy › Ads).

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with the service providers listed above, to operate the App, show ads and analyze usage, or when required by law.

6. Data retention

We keep information only for as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy. Third-party services retain data according to their own policies.

7. Your rights and choices

  • You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings.
  • You can stop all data collection by the App by uninstalling it.
  • You can ask us to access or delete data associated with your player ID by emailing us. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have additional rights, and we will honor them.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
